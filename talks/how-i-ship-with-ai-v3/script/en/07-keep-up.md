# Keep up {tag: Keep up}

How do we keep up? {#curious}Be curious. {#social}Be active on Twitter and Reddit. {#subs}Pay for subscriptions, so you can try tools and build. {#mistakes}Make mistakes, and learn.

{#cherny}Boris Cherny, who created Claude Code, says: experiment with the tools, get to know them, don't be scared of them. Just dive in, try them, be on the bleeding edge, be on the frontier.

{#view}My view: AI isn't going to replace us. I believe our field will keep growing. {#wage}In the US, median pay for software developers was [[$135,980|about one hundred thirty-six thousand dollars]] in May [[2025|twenty twenty-five]]. {#jobs}And jobs are projected to grow [[10%|ten percent]] by [[2035|twenty thirty-five]], much faster than average. {#adapt}We've lived through big changes before. We need to adapt smartly, and keep learning. Thanks, everyone!

{#ticket}Then I paste our example ClickUp ticket: filter projects by status. {#guard}Saving it means a new file. A hook catches that: no approval on record. {#around}The agent doesn't go around it. It asks me first; I approve. {#saved}It's saved word for word on a new branch, and committed.

{#grill}Then the grill. It finds the facts it can on its own. {#questions}It asks six questions, each with a recommendation. Two are for the client. {#admins}The ticket says people see only their teams' projects, but admins see everything. {#leads}And it says team leads, but the code has no team lead role. {#answer}I accept all six. {#brief}It writes a Requirement Brief with testable done criteria. Committed.

{#docs}The docs now hold the map, the ticket and the brief: this task's history. {#reads}Each new session, a hook points the agent to the map.
